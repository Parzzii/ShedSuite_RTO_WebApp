Contract files

Select one or multiple ShedSuite CSVs and/or supported RentaBarn-style contract PDFs. V7.14 converts both sources into one review session and skips duplicate Customer Order / Agreement numbers automatically.

PDF import: Digital/text-based RentaBarn-style contract PDFs are read directly; the uploaded PDF becomes that contract's combined source PDF. Scanned-image PDFs should be re-downloaded as the original digital PDF.
